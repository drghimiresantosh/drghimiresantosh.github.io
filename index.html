ABOUT DR. SANTOSH

Medicine made clearer, more personal and easier to follow.

Dr. Santosh Ghimire is a consultant physician with an MD in Internal Medicine from Patan Academy of Health Sciences. He cares for adults with both new and long-term health concerns, with experience across outpatient, inpatient, emergency and critical-care settings.

His approach is straightforward: listen carefully, explain the reasoning, and make a treatment plan that patients and families can understand and carry into daily life.
