Medical disclaimer

Important limitations of this website, appointment requests and online care.

Not emergency care

This website, its contact channels and its booking system are not emergency services. If you have severe breathing difficulty, chest pain, loss of consciousness, signs of stroke, heavy bleeding, poisoning, a severe allergic reaction, or another urgent problem, seek immediate in-person emergency care at the nearest appropriate facility.

General information only

Website content provides general health education. It does not diagnose a condition, prescribe treatment, replace an individual medical consultation, or create a doctor-patient relationship.

Appointments are not consultations

Sending a message or requesting an appointment does not constitute medical advice. A booking is confirmed only after you receive a confirmation through the scheduling or clinic communication channel.

Limits of online consultation

Some concerns cannot be assessed safely without a physical examination, vital signs, tests, complete records or urgent evaluation. Dr. Santosh may recommend a clinic visit, emergency assessment, investigation or referral instead of continuing online. Technical interruptions and incomplete information can also limit online care.

Patient responsibility

Provide accurate information about your identity, location, symptoms, medical history, medicines and allergies when requested. Do not delay urgent care while waiting for an online reply. Follow prescriptions and plans only as provided for you during a completed consultation.

No guaranteed outcome

Medical outcomes vary. No statement on this website should be understood as a promise, guarantee or claim of cure.
